Professional Community Help, When Every Minute Matters.

Chaverim of Union County delivers trusted volunteer response for roadside emergencies and urgent support situations across Union, Essex, and Hudson County, New Jersey.

What We Handle

Our team is trained for high-pressure situations and everyday support calls.

Roadside Rescue

Lockouts, jump-starts, flat tire support, fuel issues, and stuck vehicles.

Weather Response

Generator support, welfare checks, and coordination during storms.

Family Assistance

Rapid, respectful support for households in distress.

Special Circumstances

Elderly lift-assist, item retrieval, and situation-specific interventions.
